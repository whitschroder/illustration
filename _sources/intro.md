# Archaeological Illustration Instructions

The following pages are designed to assist students in learning how to use CAD and vector-based graphics software for creating archaeological illustrations for reports. The emphasis is on drawings of excavation profiles and plans, but the instructions can be adapted for other archaeological illustrations. These pages are meant only as a guide, and steps can be removed, added, or modified, or reproduced with other software. 

My preference is to begin digitizing scaled drawings using CAD software, for example [Rhinoceros 3D](https://www.rhino3d.com), where drawings can be easily scaled and drafted. Once all of the elements of the drawing are complete in Rhino 3D, I then export to [Adobe Illustrator](https://www.adobe.com/products/illustrator.html) to adjust the style of the drawing. Alternatively, drawings can be produced only in Rhino 3D or only in Adobe Illustrator without having to work with two different software packages. However, I find that the Rhino 3D interface is better suited to creating scaled drawings, while Adobe Illustrator is better suited to putting the finishing touches on drawings.
